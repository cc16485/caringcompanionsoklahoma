Getting started

From the first call to the first visit.

Most families call when they've started to worry. Here's what happens next, so you know what to expect.

  1. You call or book a visit

    Tell us about your loved one and what's been hard lately. We'll answer your questions, including cost, and set a time to come out. Call (405) 500-1758 Monday to Friday, 8 to 6, or join our opening list. Visits begin in June 2027.

  2. We visit at home, free

    We sit down with your loved one, and with you if you'd like to be there. We learn their routines, what they enjoy, what they'd like help with, and what worries the family. If they need more than companion care, we'll tell you.

  3. We write a service plan together

    The plan lists the help they'll get, the days and the hours. It's written with your loved one and the family, shared with the caregiver before the first visit, and reviewed with you at least every six months, or sooner if things change.

  4. We match a caregiver

    Every caregiver has passed a fingerprint background check through Oklahoma's OKSCREEN program. We look for someone whose personality and interests fit your loved one.

  5. Care begins, and we stay close

    Our office is in Mustang and our caregivers are supervised from there. If anything needs to change, call us any weekday and we'll update the plan.
