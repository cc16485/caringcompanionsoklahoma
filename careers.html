$20 an hour

What we'll pay companion caregivers in Mustang.

Daytime, weekdays

Monday to Friday, between 8am and 6pm. Evenings and weekends are yours.

Work that matters

You're the reason someone's week has something to look forward to.

A local team

You're supervised from our Mustang office, with people who know you by name.

The job

  • Spending time with older adults: conversation, games, walks and hobbies
  • Light housekeeping, laundry and meal preparation
  • Errands, shopping and driving clients to appointments
  • Leading a simple seated exercise routine
  • Friendly medication reminders
  • Giving family caregivers a break

This is companion work. You won't be asked to give hands-on personal care.

What you'll need

  • A kind, patient, dependable way with people
  • To pass a fingerprint background check through Oklahoma's OKSCREEN program
  • A valid driver's license and auto insurance, if you'll drive clients

What happens next

When hiring opens, we'll email you the application. It takes about two minutes, no resume needed. If it looks like a fit, we'll call you to set up an interview in Mustang.
