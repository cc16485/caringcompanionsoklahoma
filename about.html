About us

A family company, now in Mustang.

Caring Companions began in Springfield, Missouri in 2017 with one belief: people deserve to stay in their own homes for as long as they safely can, with the right support.

Where we come from

We had watched too many people move into facilities before they were ready, not because they couldn't live at home, but because they didn't have the right support. So we started helping families, one at a time.

Word spread from family to family. We're still family owned, never a franchise, and we've kept the rules that made us different. Caregivers are our own employees, never contractors, and every plan is built around the person's routines, preferences and story.

Why Mustang

Our Oklahoma office brings that same approach to Mustang and the towns around it, built around what so many working families ask for first: a dependable, friendly companion while they're at work.

You'll talk to a local team that knows your loved one by name, and every caregiver is supervised from our Mustang office.

Our promise

"We will care for your loved one with the same respect, kindness, and dignity we would want for our own family."
