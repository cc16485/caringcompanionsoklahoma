Weekday companion care in El Reno.

El Reno is about 28 minutes from our Mustang office, with a downtown full of history worth getting out to see. A regular companion for Mom or Dad, Monday to Friday between 8 and 6: good company, a hot lunch, errands done and a ride to the doctor.

What we help with in El Reno

  • Companionship: conversation, games, hobbies and walks
  • Light housekeeping, laundry and meals cooked at home
  • Errands, groceries and rides to appointments
  • A guided seated exercise routine and medication reminders
  • Respite, so the family caregiver gets a break

All of it is part of The Good Day Club, our way of helping older adults stay independent longer. Companion care is $30 an hour, starting with one 4-hour visit a week. See pricing

Questions families in El Reno ask

Do you serve all of El Reno?

We serve ZIP code 73036. If you're not sure about your address, call us at (405) 500-1758 or check your ZIP on our service area map.

When can visits start?

We're not open yet. We're getting licensed and plan to open in Mustang in June 2027. Join the opening list and we'll be in touch as opening gets closer.

Do your caregivers help with bathing or dressing?

No. Our Oklahoma license is for companion services, so our caregivers don't give hands-on personal care. If your loved one needs that, we'll tell you honestly at the first visit.

Does Medicare pay for any of this?

Regular companion care is paid privately. For families living with dementia, Medicare's GUIDE program may cover respite visits.
