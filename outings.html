How outings work

Simple, safe and planned with you.

  • Outings happen during visits, Monday to Friday between 8am and 6pm.
  • You approve each place before we go, and we write favorites into their service plan.
  • The caregiver drives and stays with them the whole time.
  • Admission, meals and class fees are paid by the family.
  • On hot or icy days, we swap in an indoor plan.

Who outings suit best

Our caregivers provide companion care, so they don't lift or physically support anyone. Outings work best for people who can walk and get in and out of a car on their own. A cane or walker is fine.
